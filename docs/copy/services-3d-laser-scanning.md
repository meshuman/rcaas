## 4. Why scan instead of measure `#why-scan`
- **H2:** Days of measuring, replaced by one walk-through
- **Body:** Measuring a building by hand means days of site visits, tape measures and sketches, and the mistakes show up later as costly changes. A laser scan records everything in one visit: every wall, beam, opening and level, including walls that aren't straight and floors that slope. If you forget a dimension, you take it from the scan, not from another site visit.

| Comparison | Tape and sketch | Handheld laser scan |
|---|---|---|
| Time on site | Days for a large building | Minutes per floor |
| What's captured | Only what you chose to measure | Everything in view |
| Irregular walls and floors | Easy to miss | Recorded as they are |
| Going back for a missed dimension | Another site visit | Measure it from the scan |
| Sharing with your team | Sketches and notes | 3D data and a shareable 3D view |

## 5. What is SLAM laser scanning? `#what-is-slam`
- **H2:** What is SLAM laser scanning?
- **Body:** SLAM stands for simultaneous localisation and mapping. The scanner fires laser pulses as our engineer walks, and works out its own position in space as it goes. The result is a dense 3D map of everything around it, captured at walking pace without setting up a tripod at each position. That makes it fast for interiors, stairs, corridors and complex structures.
- **Honest note:** Static tripod scanners can reach higher accuracy for some specialist engineering tasks, but take far longer on site. For most as-built, renovation, interior and heritage work, handheld SLAM gives the right balance of speed and accuracy. We'll tell you at the brief stage which your project needs.

## 11. Questions about 3D laser scanning `#faq`
- **H2:** Questions about 3D laser scanning

### How long does scanning take?
A single floor usually takes minutes. Most buildings are scanned in a few hours to a day, depending on size and complexity. We give you the timeline in the proposal.

### How accurate is a handheld laser scan?
Handheld SLAM LiDAR achieves millimeter-scale relative measurement accuracy across typical rooms and building levels. Where absolute georeferencing is needed, we anchor the scan to survey-grade GNSS control points. We agree the accuracy your project needs at the brief stage and tell you if it is achievable.

### Do we need to empty the building?
No. Quieter times give the cleanest results, but people passing through can usually be filtered out during point cloud processing. Having someone on site to guide us and unlock doors ensures the fastest coverage.

### Which files will I get?
We deliver industry-standard formats including E57, LAS, and LAZ for 3D point clouds, DWG and DXF for CAD drawings, and OBJ for 3D meshes. All files open directly in AutoCAD, Revit, ArchiCAD, Rhino, and CloudCompare.

### Can you scan outdoor areas and large sites too?
Yes. We combine handheld scanning with drone aerial capture for roofs, courtyards and surrounding grounds, so you get one unified model joining interior and exterior.

### Can the scan also become a 3D tour?
Yes. The same capture visit can produce both measured point cloud data for your design team and a photorealistic 3D virtual tour for your clients, saving budget and coordination time.
