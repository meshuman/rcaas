## 4. Why a 3D tour
- **H2:** More than photos, more than 360°
- **Body:** Photos show what a photographer chose. 360° tours let people look around, but only from fixed spots. A 3D tour gives them the whole place, at real scale, to explore in their own way. That's how people get a true feel for a room, a campus or a courtyard before they decide to visit.

### Comparison table
| Feature | Photos & video | 360° photo tour | 3D virtual tour |
|---|---|---|---|
| How people explore | Watch what you show them | Look around from fixed points | Move freely through the space |
| Sense of size and layout | Limited | Partial | Close to being there |
| Best at | Mood, emotion, social media | Quick, low-cost overviews | Helping people decide with confidence |
| Spatial freedom | Fixed viewpoint | Bubble jumps only | Six degrees of freedom (6DoF) continuous exploration |
| In-scene measurement | Not possible | Inaccurate or unavailable | Millimetre-accurate real scale measurements |
| Secondary uses | Media gallery only | Virtual tour only | VR headset ready, fly-through films, architectural CAD/BIM data |

- **Note under table:** The best results often combine them. From one capture we can also produce fly-through films and social clips. See Visual Storytelling.

## 7. How we make it `#process`

## 8. Frequently asked questions `#faq`
- **H2:** Frequently asked questions

### How is a 3D virtual tour different from a 360° photo tour?
A 360° photo tour consists of static panoramic spheres; you click and jump between predetermined dots, looking around like a swivel chair. A 3D virtual tour is an actual continuous three-dimensional reconstruction. Visitors move freely in any direction (six degrees of freedom), feeling true depth, ceiling heights and architectural flow.

### How fast does the tour load on mobile connections in Nepal?
We utilise progressive spatial streaming. The base geometry and closest room perspectives load in less than two seconds, even over standard 4G and mobile networks in Kathmandu and regional districts. Higher detail streams progressively as the visitor navigates.

### Do visitors need to download an application or plugin?
No. Tours run entirely inside modern mobile and desktop web browsers (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). There are no apps to install and no plugins required.

### Can we embed the 3D tour on our existing website?
Yes. We supply an easy one-line embed code (iframe) that works with WordPress, Webflow, Squarespace, Wix, Shopify or custom websites. It is fully responsive and adjusts automatically to mobile screens.

### Can we update hotspots and booking links after launch?
Yes. Hotspots, text descriptions, prices, phone numbers and booking URLs are managed in our cloud console and can be updated at any time without re-scanning the property.

### How much preparation does our space require before capture?
Treat capture day like a professional photoshoot: ensure lighting is on, spaces are tidy, clutter is removed, and pathways are clear. We provide a straightforward preparation checklist prior to our visit.
