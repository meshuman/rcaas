5. How to prepare
H2: How to prepare for capture day
Choose the spaces that matter most. Think about what your audience needs to see to decide.
Tidy and stage. Clear clutter, make beds, turn on lights, open curtains.
Plan for quiet. Fewer people moving through gives the cleanest result. Weekends, holidays or early mornings often work well.
Arrange access. Keys, doors, rooftops and any restricted areas.
Share what you have. Existing drawings, your logo and brand colours, and where the experience will be used.
Tell people. Let staff, guests or students know that capture is happening.
For heritage sites: confirm who gives permission, and any rules or times to avoid.

6. Questions to ask any provider
H2: Questions to ask before you hire anyone
Question
Why it matters
Is it a true 3D tour or a 360° photo tour?
They are different products with different results.
Can I see a live example on my phone?
The best test is how it feels to your audience.
Who owns the files, and what happens if hosting ends?
Avoid losing your experience later.
How are people and faces handled?
Protect guests, students and staff.
Can I embed it on my website and add booking or enquiry buttons?
The tour should lead to action.
Can it be updated after a renovation?
Places change; your experience should too.
If I need measurements, how accurate is the data?
Visual tours and measured data are not the same.
