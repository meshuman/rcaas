# Privacy Policy: notes for legal review

Page: /privacy/ (src/pages/PrivacyPage.tsx). Source draft: docs/copy/privacy.md.
Status: draft, marked noindex. Prepared 8 October 2026 by the website team. This is not legal advice; please confirm or correct each point below.

## 1. Statutory references to confirm (section 5 of the policy)

The policy now names:

- Constitution of Nepal (2015), Article 28 (right to privacy)
- The Privacy Act, 2075 (2018) (वैयक्तिक गोपनीयता सम्बन्धी ऐन, २०७५)
- The Privacy Regulations, 2077 (2020)

Please confirm the titles and citations, and advise whether to also name:

- Electronic Transactions Act, 2063 (2008): website and electronic records
- National Penal (Code) Act, 2074 (2017): privacy-related offences, including photographing people without consent
- Children's Act, 2075 (2018): capture at schools and other places with children

## 2. Points where the law may require more than the draft says

1. **Consent and purpose.** The Privacy Act is understood to require consent to collect personal information, and to limit its use to the stated purpose. The policy now states purpose limitation explicitly. Please confirm whether "performance of a contract" and "legal obligation" are valid grounds on their own under Nepali law, or whether consent should be obtained in every case.
2. **People in captures.** Scans and aerial images can include identifiable people, number plates and neighbouring properties. The policy now says the client is responsible for informing people on site. Please advise whether RCAAS also needs signage, written consent for identifiable people, or a blurring commitment (still [[TBI]], Register H03) before publishing.
3. **Children.** The policy now says schools are asked to inform parents or guardians before capture. Please confirm whether guardian consent is required, and whether blurring every identifiable child should be a firm commitment.
4. **Sensitive information.** The policy asks people not to send sensitive information. Please confirm the categories the Privacy Act treats as sensitive, and correct the list if needed.
5. **Cross-border storage.** Hosting, email, analytics and fonts providers may store data outside Nepal. Please advise whether consent or other safeguards are required for transfers abroad.
6. **Retention.** Please advise statutory minimums, in particular for tax and accounting records, before the [[TBI]] periods are filled in.
7. **Complaints.** The policy says complaints can be raised "under the laws of Nepal" ([[TBC]]). Please name the authority or route, if one applies.
8. **Language.** Please advise whether a Nepali version is advisable, and which version should prevail.

## 3. New commitments added for RCAAS to approve

These are standard good practice, but each is a promise RCAAS must be able to keep:

- deleting or anonymising information after the retention periods
- telling affected people (and authorities where required) about a security incident likely to affect them
- deleting information a child has sent through the website
- verifying identity before acting on a rights request, and replying within [[TBI: response period]]
- highlighting significant changes to the policy on the page
- naming a person or role responsible for privacy ([[TBC]])

## 4. Facts the policy now states about the website

Checked against the site code on 8 October 2026:

- Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives each visitor's IP address. Self-hosting the fonts would remove this.
- WhatsApp, LinkedIn and Google Maps are linked, not embedded.
- The website sets no cookies and uses no browser storage. No analytics tool is installed yet. When one is added, update section 3, section 6 and the cookie [[TBC]] together.
