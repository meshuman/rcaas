## 4. Why story `#why-story`
- **H2:** A place on its own is information. A place with a story is a reason to act.
- **Body:** People don't remember a list of rooms or a floor area. They remember how a place made them feel and the one moment that convinced them. Before we produce anything, we answer four questions with you:

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Who is watching? | A parent abroad and a local student need different stories. |
| What should they feel? | Calm, pride, excitement, trust. The feeling shapes the pace, light and sound. |
| What should they notice? | The view from the room, the new lab, the carving no one else has shown. |
| What should they do next? | Book, apply, visit, invest or support. Every story ends with that step. |

## 8. Frequently asked questions `#faq`
- **H2:** Frequently asked questions

### Do we need a film crew on site?
No traditional film crew with tracks, booms or lighting rigs is needed. Our capture team needs only 2 to 6 hours on site with compact laser and camera equipment. All camera angles, crane shots and lighting are crafted in virtual cinematography from the accurate 3D model.

### Can you deliver videos formatted for both Instagram/TikTok and YouTube/Websites?
Yes. Every capture can be rendered in 16:9 widescreen, 9:16 vertical reels/TikToks, and 1:1 squares from the exact same spatial model.

### Can we update the story later without re-scanning?
Yes. If your branding, message, voiceover or music changes, we re-render new camera movements and graphics directly from the existing 3D digital model without re-visiting the physical site.

### What languages do you support for voiceover?
We produce professional narration in British English, American English, and Nepali, with subtitles in any language.
