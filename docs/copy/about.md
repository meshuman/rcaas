6. What guides our work #values
H2: What guides our work
Value
Line
Accuracy comes first
An experience is only worth trusting if it is true to the place. We measure, check and stay honest about what the data can do.
Every experience has a purpose
We design around the action you want people to take, not around the technology.
Seeing shouldn't depend on being there
A student abroad, a traveller planning a trip or a researcher in another city should be able to experience a place properly.
Record places before they change
Buildings and heritage are lost to time, weather and development. We would rather document too early than too late.
Heritage belongs to everyone
Our records should be useful to researchers, visible to the public and respectful to the communities who care for them.
Good work is built together
Clients and partners bring the purpose; we bring the craft. The results are better for both.
Trust is earned
We take on work we can do well, finish what we start and learn from every project.
