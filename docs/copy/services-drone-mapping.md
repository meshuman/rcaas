## 4. Why drone mapping `#why-drone`
- **H2:** The whole site, mapped in a fraction of the time
- **Body:** Some places can't be measured well from the ground: large sites, steep terrain, rooftops and courtyards hidden behind walls. A survey drone captures them in a few planned flights, without anyone climbing a roof or walking every slope. The images become a true-scale map you can measure from, and a 3D model of the ground and everything on it.

| Comparison | Ground survey alone | Drone mapping |
|---|---|---|
| Large or steep sites | Slow, many instrument setups | Covered in planned autonomous flights |
| Roofs and courtyards | Hard, slow or unsafe to reach | Captured safely from above |
| What's recorded | Selected discrete points | The whole visible surface at sub-cm detail |
| Repeat surveys | Full manual effort each time | Same flight plan, easy to repeat |

- **Honest note:** Drones see surfaces. Under dense trees or heavy vegetation, the ground itself may not be visible, so we combine aerial capture with ground survey where the terrain beneath canopy matters. We'll advise at the brief stage.

## 11. Questions about drone mapping `#faq`
- **H2:** Questions about drone mapping

### Do you need permission to fly?
Yes. Drone flights in Nepal require regulatory approvals from civil aviation and local administration authorities. We plan flight permits and clearance lead times into your project schedule, handling the documentation on your behalf.

### How large an area can you map?
We can map sites ranging from a single building compound to hundreds of hectares. Terrain slope, elevation altitude, required ground resolution, and airspace conditions all determine daily coverage. We provide exact flight schedules in your proposal.

### How accurate is drone mapping?
With survey-grade GNSS ground control points (GCPs) and RTK positioning, horizontal and vertical accuracy is typically within 2 to 5 centimeters. Without ground control, absolute accuracy depends on onboard GNSS, while relative scale remains highly consistent. We agree target tolerances at the brief stage.

### Can you fly over temples and heritage sites?
Yes, with the appropriate government permissions and with complete respect for sacred spaces, local guthi trusts, and worshippers. Drone capture is 100% contactless, ensuring no physical impact on historic monuments.

### What if the weather is bad?
We track local micro-climates closely. Flights are never conducted in heavy rain, active thunderstorms, or excessive wind speeds exceeding manufacturer safety envelopes. We build weather contingency buffer days into all field schedules.

### Can you combine aerial and ground data?
Yes. That is one of our core specialties. We combine drone mapping with handheld SLAM laser scanning, joining high-altitude roof and compound topography with intricate interior floor plans into one seamless 3D coordinate model.
