# Terms of Use: notes for legal review

Page: /terms/ (src/pages/TermsPage.tsx). Source draft: docs/copy/terms.md.
Status: draft, marked noindex. Prepared 8 October 2026 by the website team. This is not legal advice; please confirm or correct each point below.

These are website terms of use only. Project terms (scope, payment, ownership of deliverables, hosting, revisions) belong in proposals and client contracts.

## 1. Statutory references to confirm

The terms now name:

- Copyright Act, 2059 (2002): cited in section 3 of the terms (protection of website content)
- Consumer Protection Act, 2075 (2018): cited in section 7 of the terms (consumer rights that cannot be excluded)

Please confirm both, and advise whether to also refer to:

- Electronic Transactions Act, 2063 (2008): unauthorised access to computer systems (relevant to section 2 of the terms)
- Muluki Civil Code, 2074 (2017) or the Contract Act, 2056 (2000): whether acceptance by browsing ("by using the website, you agree") is enforceable, or whether key terms should be accepted actively (for example, a checkbox on forms)

## 2. Points to advise on

1. **Acceptance by use (sections 1 and 9).** Please advise how enforceable browse-only acceptance and "continued use means you accept the updated terms" are under Nepali law. The terms now also say that the version in force at the time of use applies, and that significant changes will be highlighted.
2. **Limitation of liability (section 7).** The draft adds an "as is" statement and preserves consumer rights. Please confirm the wording is effective, and whether any liability (for example, for negligence) cannot be excluded.
3. **Jurisdiction (section 10).** Please confirm "the courts of Kathmandu", or the correct court (for example, Kathmandu District Court).
4. **Scraping and AI training (section 2).** Prohibiting automated collection is added. Whether to also prohibit AI training depends on the robots.txt decision (Register G13). Please advise whether a terms clause is enforceable against crawlers, alongside robots.txt.
5. **Misuse of 3D experiences (section 2).** Added: not using an experience to plan unauthorised entry to, or harm at, a place shown. This matters for hotels, schools and heritage sites. Please confirm the wording.
6. **Client content (section 3).** Experiences of client places are shown "with their permission". Please confirm that client contracts grant RCAAS a licence to display them on the website, and what happens when a client withdraws permission.
7. **Trade marks (section 3).** The site names equipment and software makers (for example, XGRIDS and DJI on /how-we-work/). The terms now say these names identify products only and imply no endorsement. Please confirm this is sufficient.
8. **Precedence (section 1).** Project contracts take priority over these terms if they conflict. Please confirm this matches the client contract wording.
9. **Language (section 11).** If a Nepali version is published, please advise which version should prevail.
