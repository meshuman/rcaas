## 2. The three pillars

### 2.1 Immersive Experiences
- **H2:** Immersive Experiences
- **Promise:** Let people step inside.
- **Body:** Photorealistic 3D tours that open from a link, VR that puts visitors on site, and interactive experiences built with game-engine tools.
- **Helps you:** Win bookings, applications, sales enquiries and visits.
- **Includes:**
  - 3D virtual tours → `/services/immersive-experiences/3d-virtual-tours/`
  - VR experiences → `/services/immersive-experiences/#vr`
  - AR experiences → `/services/immersive-experiences/#ar`
  - Interactive experiences → `/services/immersive-experiences/#interactive`
- **Link:** Explore immersive experiences → `/services/immersive-experiences/`

### 2.2 Visual Storytelling
- **H2:** Visual Storytelling
- **Promise:** Turn your place into a story.
- **Body:** Guided tours, cinematic fly-through films and social content made from your 3D capture, shaped around what you want your audience to feel and do.
- **Helps you:** Run campaigns, raise awareness, attract funding and engage the public.
- **Includes:**
  - Guided and narrated tours → `/services/visual-storytelling/#guided-tours`
  - Fly-through films and renders → `/services/visual-storytelling/#films`
  - Social content → `/services/visual-storytelling/#social`
  - Exhibition and event content → `/services/visual-storytelling/#exhibitions`
- **Link:** Explore visual storytelling → `/services/visual-storytelling/`

### 2.3 Digital Twins & Survey
- **H2:** Digital Twins & Survey
- **Promise:** Measure, design and plan from reality.
- **Body:** Accurate 3D records of buildings, sites and landscapes, captured with advanced laser and aerial scanning, ready for design, planning and preservation.
- **Helps you:** Design with confidence, cut repeat site visits, plan better and preserve what matters.
- **Includes:**
  - 3D laser scanning → `/services/digital-twins/3d-laser-scanning/`
  - Drone mapping and aerial survey → `/services/digital-twins/drone-mapping/`
  - As-built drawings and CAD/BIM-ready data → `/services/digital-twins/#as-built`
  - Survey and GIS → `/services/digital-twins/#survey-gis`
- **Link:** Explore digital twins → `/services/digital-twins/`

## 4. Which is right for you? `#choose`
- **H2:** Which is right for you?
- **Intro:** Start with your goal. We'll recommend the mix.

| Your goal | Best fit | Example of what you get |
|---|---|---|
| More bookings or visits | Immersive Experiences | A 3D tour on your website and booking page |
| More applications or enrolments | Immersive Experiences + Visual Storytelling | A campus tour and a short film for admissions campaigns |
| Sell or lease property | Immersive Experiences + Visual Storytelling | A 3D walkthrough of a show flat and fly-through clips for ads |
| Design, renovate or fit out | Digital Twins & Survey | A measured 3D scan and drawings for your design software |
| Preserve heritage | Digital Twins & Survey + Immersive Experiences | A measured record for conservators and a public 3D experience |
| Plan or engage the public | Digital Twins & Survey + Visual Storytelling | 3D base data for planning and visuals residents understand |
